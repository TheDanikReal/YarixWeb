PROJECT BRIEF

A dangerous run for freedom.

The Semiradical is a game that I’m planning to work on after finishing other projects. You play as Yaromir, a risky 20-year-old lectreido who is fast enough to challenge an army of robots.

Drawings on this page are prototypes, not the final visual style.

Plot

Zinoviy is going to take over the world and enslave the whole lectreido race. Yaromir is very mad about this and decides to take risks for freedom.

The game begins in Belarus in the first quarter of the 21st century.

Related project

ALTRADICAL shares Yaromir and other general ideas with The Semiradical, but it is based on the game and is not canon.

CAST

Meet the resistance

Yaromir

A risky 20-year-old lectreido with the ability to run at crazy speed from birth. He created his own weapons for the adventure: a sword and a blaster.

Zinoviy

A mad human genius who wants to take over the world. He has ruined streets with his robots and other “garbage”. His description and design are still in development.

Development log

Concept

The concept originated with a dream game in 2022. Its original name was “Project: Wetdarum”, set on a fictional planet. The target visual style is hand-drawn, though it began as 16-bit pixel art.

Inspiration

  • Sonic The Hedgehog
  • Pizza Tower

Engine forecast

The game will use its own engine, probably written in C with the SDL library instead of a ready-made engine.
